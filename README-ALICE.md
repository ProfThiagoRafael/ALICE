# ALICE — Commercial Client Prospecting Agent

**AI Solutions Architecture | Multi-Agent Systems | Business Process Automation | Systems Integration**

ALICE is an AI-assisted B2B commercial workflow concept designed to help organize business prospecting activities and support responsible customer communication.

It is part of a modular multi-agent initiative in which specialized agents have clearly separated responsibilities:

- **LIZ** focuses on business research and the organization of prospect information.
- **ALICE** focuses on the commercial workflow and communication process.

The public repository presents the project, its goals, and its high-level architecture. It intentionally does not publish executable workflows, source code, prompts, private configuration, or operational procedures.

## Project vision

The project explores how AI agents and workflow automation can support a structured commercial process while keeping data acquisition, business validation, and customer communication as distinct responsibilities.

## Project objectives

- Organize business prospect information.
- Support structured and reviewable commercial workflows.
- Apply validation and responsible-use checks.
- Explore AI-assisted communication.
- Improve operational traceability and process management.
- Maintain a modular architecture that can evolve over time.

## Responsible use and data protection

The project is intended to be developed and operated in accordance with applicable laws and platform policies, including Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018), where applicable.

Responsible operation requires appropriate legal grounds for data processing, transparent data provenance, access controls, respect for opt-out requests, and protection of personal information.

Public availability of business information does not, by itself, establish permission for unsolicited marketing communication.
